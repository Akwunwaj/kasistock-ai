# Three-Minute Demonstration Script

## 0:00-0:20 - The problem

A small retailer has R1,500, limited shelf stock, two inconsistent supplier lists and 30 days of
sales. The decision is what to buy now without wasting scarce cash.

## 0:20-0:45 - Evidence authority

Show the accepted shelf, supplier and sales evidence. Explain that GPT-5.6 interprets images and
documents, but uncertain values require human acceptance and a hash-valid evidence snapshot.

## 0:45-1:10 - Product identity

Load the prepared evidence in `/decision`. Show barcode and alias matches, then confirm an uncertain
fuzzy match. Freeze the mapping set and point to its SHA-256 hash.

## 1:10-1:40 - Deterministic decision engine

Run the R1,500 scenario. Show daily velocity, days of cover, reorder need and supplier price
comparisons. Emphasise that all financial calculations use deterministic integer-cent code.

## 1:40-2:05 - Merchant edits and approval

Adjust one pack quantity. Create the signed approval draft. Show the budget remaining and expected
margin, tick the explicit confirmation statement and approve.

## 2:05-2:35 - Business outputs

Show the two supplier-specific orders. Download one approved PDF and copy its WhatsApp-ready
supplier message. Point out that no purchase order exists before merchant approval.

## 2:35-2:55 - Audit and evaluation

Show the six-event audit timeline and the measured 12-case identity evaluation: 91.67% accuracy and
zero unsafe automatic merges.

## 2:55-3:00 - Closing line

KasiStock AI turns messy retail evidence and limited cash into an explainable, approved and
supplier-ready stock decision.
