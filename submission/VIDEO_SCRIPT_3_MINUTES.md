# Three-minute demonstration script

Target duration: **2 minutes 35 seconds**. Keep the final public video below three minutes.

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem

**Visual:** Home dashboard and R1,500 budget.

**Voice-over:**

> Small retailers often restock from a shelf check, inconsistent supplier lists, and a fixed amount of cash. KasiStock AI answers one practical question: what should I buy today, from which supplier, without exceeding my budget?

## 0:15–0:45 — Multimodal evidence

**Visual:** Open `/evidence`, load the prepared demonstration, and show one high-confidence and one review-required observation.

**Voice-over:**

> GPT-5.6 extracts structured facts from shelf images and supplier documents. But the model has no purchasing authority. Uncertain observations are visible, and the merchant must verify them before they become accepted evidence.

## 0:45–1:15 — Product reconciliation

**Visual:** Open `/decision`, load prepared evidence, and show the sixteen source labels and uncertain mappings.

**Voice-over:**

> Product descriptions vary across shelves, sales records, and suppliers. Deterministic rules resolve barcodes, governed aliases, and exact names first. Fuzzy or GPT-assisted candidates remain blocked until a person confirms them.

## 1:15–1:50 — Deterministic decision

**Visual:** Accept mappings, build the plan, and show supplier comparison, days of cover, and budget allocation.

**Voice-over:**

> Once the evidence is accepted, deterministic TypeScript calculates sales velocity, days of cover, effective supplier costs, pack constraints, and the budget-constrained order. Currency uses integer cents, and identical inputs produce identical results.

## 1:50–2:20 — Approval and outputs

**Visual:** Create the signed draft, tick the approval checkbox, approve, and show the two supplier purchase orders.

**Voice-over:**

> The server rebuilds and signs the draft. The merchant explicitly approves R1,399.10, leaving R100.90. KasiStock AI then creates two supplier-specific PDFs, two WhatsApp-ready messages, and six immutable audit events.

## 2:20–2:35 — Engineering evidence

**Visual:** Architecture page, evaluation result, and test summary.

**Voice-over:**

> Codex helped build the architecture, contracts, tests, evaluations, accessibility fixes, and submission release. The result is trustworthy AI by separation of authority: the model interprets, deterministic code calculates, and the merchant decides.

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1440×900 or 1920×1080.
- Use browser zoom at 100%.
- Hide bookmarks, notifications, API keys, and local file paths.
- Use the prepared workflow for reliability.
- Keep cursor movement deliberate.
- Add captions.
- Upload publicly or unlisted to YouTube as permitted by the competition rules.
- Verify the published duration remains below three minutes.
